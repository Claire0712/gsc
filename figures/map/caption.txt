Population-weighted local temperature exceedance on days without country-level daily exceedance, 2022–2025. Colour shows the within-country mean fraction of fixed 2025 population weights whose ERA5 cell UTC Tmax strictly exceeds that cell’s 2018–2021 calendar-window Q90, conditional on the original country UTC Tmax not exceeding its frozen country Q90. Reference windows span ±15 calendar days and require at least 112 observations from all four years. Every supported day within a country receives equal weight; country-specific denominators range from 781 to 1394 days. This daily classification is not the warm-season two-day/network-qualified event definition. The 0–15% colour domain covers all observed country means (maximum below 12.5%); daily fractions themselves may range from 0 to 100%. Gray marks the four entities without fixed population weights; zero remains a valid measured value. Equal Earth projection (EPSG:8857), retained exact-ID geoBoundaries ADM0 display polygons, simplified at 0.08° only for rendering. The map shows country summaries, not pixel-level geography, outage footprints, independent observations, a global population-weighted ranking or a causal effect. No new geographic data were downloaded.
